# Price, Ownership Transfer, Payment and Handover

## Comparable-listing price method

1. Choose >=5 currently active nearby listings where possible, preferably 8–12. Same make/model/trim/fuel/transmission first; same registration/manufacture years +/-2; mileage within roughly +/-30% or explicitly adjust.
2. Collect URL, observed date/time, list source, year, variant, mileage, fuel, owners claimed, city, asking price, visible defects and source evidence.
3. Distinguish: asking vs sale price (sold data generally unavailable), dealer vs private, asking age, optional coverage/warranty, road tax and paperwork.
4. Median asking price, low/high credible asking range and price per kilometre/year differences are evidence; **do not claim a fixed negotiation discount**. Pricing adjustments are scenario assumptions, not 'official value'.
5. Add a written immediate-repair estimate, tyres/battery/major service, legal compliance, PUC/HSRP, tax/fees, title risk and insurance. Avoid fake precision.
6. Only if the user asks for a buying judgment: Present scenario table: verified clean/roadworthy/transferable, minor known fixes, unresolved major repairs, unreconciled legal title; explicitly show unknowns.

## Transfer and loan

- For normal sale, official Parivahan lists Form 29 transferor notice and Form 30 transferee application; timelines and supporting documents may vary with intrastate/interstate status. Do not submit transfer before confirming the seller's title and signatures. Official guide: https://mparivahan.parivahan.gov.in/mstatic/english/rc-info-ownership.html
- If vehicle has current/old hypothecation, inspect financier entry and obtain lender NOC/dues-clearance and Form 35 only for termination when relevant. Official: https://mparivahan.parivahan.gov.in/mstatic/english/rc-info-hp-termination.html
- An interstate transfer or re-registration can require NOC/Form 28 or tax differential; use state/RTO rules. A same-state sale does not automatically need interstate NOC.
- Compare seller's claimed identity (privately) with RC owner, ensure dealer has authorization to sell, and get signed transfer undertaking with timeline, vehicle VIN/plate, outstanding claim/penalty responsibility and accurate delivery date/time.
- Check outstanding traffic challans in national and state portals. For inaccessible owner's OTP, seller must obtain proof. Note that insurer/no-claim bonuses and policy transfer rules vary; buyer should arrange insurance in own name.
- Do not transfer purchase price in full on a mere 'applied' receipt. If any deposit is requested, get signed refund conditions and proof of recipient authority. No surprise third-party beneficiary.
- On handover get both keys, service book, vehicle manuals, RC, insurance papers, PUC, tyre spare/tools, audio/GPS access reset, signed invoice/sale receipt, odometer/condition photo, and transfer acknowledgment.

## Avoid misstatements

- `Owner count` is unknown until an actual number or history shown; 1 visible owner name is not proof of sole ownership.
- `No lien found` only if official hypothecation record affirmatively states none; blank form is not evidence.
- `Accident history 1` counts an indicator, not severity or repair cost.
- Manufacturer recall check and independent workshop reports are separate.
- Do not assign an accident-free / clean title based solely on an online absence of hits.
- Quote exact policy/fee effective dates; tax, renewal and environmental fees can change.
