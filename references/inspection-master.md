# Detailed Master Physical and Document Inspection Checklist

Check each item with a reliable source or qualified inspector. **Verified** means direct evidence was examined; a photo alone cannot certify roadworthiness. Checks can be Not checked, Verified, Issue, Blocked or Not applicable. Fuel-specific checks only apply when fitted.

## RED-FLAG REVIEW — SCREEN FIRST

- **V001 — Registration / fitness expired** — Review the official RC/registration validity date and any expired fitness/registration status.
- **V002 — RC renewal not processed** — Identify any renewal application paid online but still pending/not inwarded at the RTO.
- **V003 — Vehicle status non-compliant** — Investigate any NTR/VAHAN blacklist or non-compliant status and its cause.
- **V004 — Accident-history record** — If a portal shows an accident count, obtain the actual event date, source and repair details.
- **V005 — Motor-vehicle tax validity** — Compare current tax-validity field with payment transaction history and arrears.
- **V006 — Pollution certificate expired** — Check current official PUC expiry and obtain certificate or reason not available.
- **V007 — HSRP record** — Check official HSRP record and physical plates where required.
- **V008 — Previous owner / finance history** — Check previous owner count, financier and any transfer/loan history.
- **V009 — Odometer history** — Compare dashboard kilometres with historic service and inspection readings.
- **V010 — Steering-wheel emblem** — Check any steering wheel/branded component mismatch, SRS airbag and horn.

## REGISTRATION, OWNERSHIP AND DOCUMENTS

- **V011 — Original RC or official electronic RC** — Match registration, vehicle model/variant variant, fuel, full VIN, engine no. and registered owner.
- **V012 — Current approved RC expiry** — Obtain current approved renewal/validity, not just the fee receipt.
- **V013 — Owner identity and authority** — Verify identity of the registered owner or a written authority to sell.
- **V014 — Ownership count / transfers** — Obtain official previous-owner count and transfer history; presently unknown.
- **V015 — Physical VIN stamping** — Compare the full 17-character metal marking with the RC and receipt.
- **V016 — Physical engine-number marking** — Find and compare the complete engine number with registered details.
- **V017 — Active bank hypothecation** — Verify whether a financier or loan is recorded on the registration.
- **V018 — Loan closure, if applicable** — Request lender NOC, Form 35 and removal of hypothecation from RC.
- **V019 — Vehicle blacklist or hold** — Check any police/RTO hold, blacklist or restriction on vehicle services.
- **V020 — National and state challans** — Record national no-match result; Maharashtra owner-OTP check remains incomplete.
- **V021 — Insurance policy and identity** — Match current insurance policy, expiry and registration; review coverage terms.
- **V022 — PUC, HSRP and tax records** — Check current PUC, plate fitment, MV tax dues and supporting receipts.

## DOCUMENTS & ONLINE LOOKUPS

- **V109 — Manufacturing month/year** — Check build date plate or OEM invoice; registration date can differ from date of manufacture.
- **V110 — Exact variant and safety equipment** — Compare registered trim, fitted features, engine, airbags and ABS to OEM brochure and VIN-specific build records.
- **V111 — Loan or other liens across owner changes** — If hypothecation was ever present, check official removal and documented financier clearance.
- **V112 — Commercial/taxi use and vehicle class** — Verify current and previous registered class where documented; private-use claim must align with documents.

## HISTORY, ACCIDENT AND BODY INSPECTION

- **V023 — Previous dated odometer readings** — Compare the displayed odometer with historical service bills, inspection records and invoices.
- **V024 — Manufacturer-authorised workshop history** — Request dated service and body-repair records from a workshop independently chosen by the buyer.
- **V025 — Insurance claim history** — Request owner-authorised claim, loss assessment and cashless repairs data.
- **V026 — NTR accident-history detail** — Obtain particulars for any accident history count, including date and damage.
- **V027 — Front chassis rails / apron** — Inspect crash members, radiator support, joints and repair welds.
- **V028 — Rear crash structure / boot floor** — Remove spare-wheel cover; inspect seams, distortion and replacement panels.
- **V029 — Roof, A/B/C pillars** — Inspect structural areas for weld repairs, filler and alignment.
- **V030 — Door, bonnet and boot alignment** — Compare panel gaps, bolts, sealant and opening/closing operation.
- **V031 — Paint-thickness and matching** — Look for repaint and filler differences; repaint alone is not proof of a crash.
- **V032 — Flood and water ingress** — Inspect seat rails, footwells, under-carpets, connectors and corrosion.
- **V033 — VIN plate / stamping integrity** — Check tampering, substituted tags, grinding and unusual re-stamping.
- **V034 — Steering assembly / SRS** — Identify non-original wheel/emblem if observed; verify airbag system and horn.
- **V113 — Previous listing and auction photos** — Search exact VIN/plate for older sales, salvage, dealer photos and repeated kilometre claims.
- **V114 — Airbag deployment and SRS parts** — Check airbag presence and original fitment, mounting points, trim, seat pretensioners and SRS DTCs.
- **V115 — Tyre age vs odometer claim** — Compare tyre DOT age and wear to claimed usage; normal replacements are not evidence of tampering.

## RTO FITNESS / RC RENEWAL - PROCESS

- **V035 — Form 25 renewal application** — Ask for completed/acknowledged renewal application and required attachments.
- **V036 — Application inward / acceptance** — Verify the recorded renewal application was formally received and processed by MH-01.
- **V037 — Vehicle inspection appointment** — Obtain the inspection booking, test date and inspecting authority.
- **V038 — Fitness / roadworthiness inspection** — Request an actual dated inspection result; fee payment alone is not an inspection.
- **V039 — Accepted fitness test result** — Obtain recorded pass/fail outcome and any defect or reinspection notice.
- **V040 — Approved renewed RC** — Verify approval, new validity date and vehicle registration status in VAHAN.
- **V041 — RC printing or electronic issue** — Obtain updated RC; check application print/dispatch status.
- **V042 — Current tax/arrears clearance** — Reconcile the latest MV Tax payment with the registration validity field.
- **V043 — Current valid PUC** — Obtain a new certificate and confirm online expiry and vehicle identity.
- **V044 — Insurance certificate** — Present valid insurance matching registration and owner as needed.
- **V045 — Chassis/engine pencil print** — Prepare if required by local RTO, matching the physical vehicle markings.
- **V046 — HSRP fitment or compliance** — Check physical plate installation and any necessary fitment/order status.

## FITNESS - LIGHTS, GLASS AND PASSENGER SAFETY

- **V047 — Low/high beam and headlight aim** — Test both sides, dip/high switching and beam aim; replace damaged lenses.
- **V048 — Brake, tail and indicator lamps** — Verify brake lights, both turn signals, hazards and tail lamps.
- **V049 — Reverse, parking and number-plate lights** — Check reverse lamps, parking lamps and plate illumination.
- **V050 — Reflectors and exterior visibility** — Check lenses, reflectors and conspicuity without broken or missing parts.
- **V051 — Windscreen and safety glass** — Check cracks, major chips, tinted alterations and glass installation.
- **V052 — Wipers and washer jets** — Check front wipe pattern, blades, washer spray and motor speeds.
- **V053 — Rear-view and side mirrors** — Verify both mirrors are secure, undamaged and adjustable.
- **V054 — Horn** — Confirm reliable operation and appropriate sound.
- **V055 — Speedometer and dashboard gauges** — Verify speedometer, odometer, temperature gauge and warning lights.
- **V056 — Front and rear seat belts** — Verify all fitted belts latch, retract and show no fraying or alteration.
- **V057 — Seat mountings and head restraints** — Check front/rear seat anchors, rails and restraints where fitted.
- **V058 — Doors, bonnet and boot latches** — Ensure secure latching; check child locks and central locking.
- **V059 — Registration plates** — Check secure readable plate and correct registration; inspect HSRP fitment.
- **V060 — Tyres including spare** — Check size, damage, tread depth, tyre age and available spare/tools.
- **V116 — High-mounted brake light** — Verify centre high-mounted stop lamp where factory-equipped and its wiring.
- **V117 — Child safety seats and ISOFIX** — Inspect available child-seat anchors and seat belt usability if transporting children.

## FITNESS - BRAKES, STEERING, EMISSIONS AND CHASSIS

- **V061 — Service brake performance** — Verify even effective braking; request brake-tester reading if available.
- **V062 — Parking / handbrake efficiency** — Confirm secure holding and inspection of rear-brake operation.
- **V063 — Brake lines, fluid and hoses** — Inspect leakage, corrosion, worn flexible hoses and fluid condition.
- **V064 — Steering free play and joints** — Check tie rods, steering rack, play, play-related knocks and mounts.
- **V065 — Suspension and shock absorbers** — Inspect springs, bushes, struts, dampers and wheel alignment.
- **V066 — Wheel hubs, bearings and fasteners** — Check abnormal play, bearing noise, wheel studs and lug nuts.
- **V067 — Chassis / subframe integrity** — Inspect frame, subframes, cross-members, corrosion and repairs on lift.
- **V068 — Exhaust, silencer and mounting** — Check leakage, excess noise, loose hangers and exhaust position.
- **V069 — Petrol emissions and PUC test** — Measure regulated exhaust pollutants and obtain valid current PUC.
- **V070 — Fuel system and tank** — Inspect fuel lines, clamps, tank, filler neck and leakage.
- **V071 — Engine / transmission fluid leaks** — Check engine, gearbox, steering and coolant leaks from underneath.
- **V072 — Speedometer/side-slip/headlamp tests** — Request instrument test outputs if performed at test station.
- **V073 — Battery/alternator charging** — Check starting and charging voltage, terminals and earth connections.
- **V074 — Airbag and ABS self-check** — Confirm warning lamps self-test normally; use OBD where supported.
- **V118 — Tyre sidewall damage and pressures** — Check bulges, cracks, puncture repair, matching tyres and correct cold pressures.
- **V119 — Headlamp tester or alignment** — Request beam aim test if available and inspect mounts for damage.
- **V120 — ABS and brake warning diagnostics** — Confirm normal bulb check and no persistent ABS/brake fault on engine-running test.

## ENGINE, GEARBOX AND ROAD TEST

- **V075 — Cold engine start** — Start cold; inspect starting effort, smoke, rattling and unusual vibration.
- **V076 — Idling and acceleration** — Check idling stability, engine sounds and smooth acceleration.
- **V077 — Cooling system under load** — Warm the engine, test fan operation and monitor overheating or coolant loss.
- **V078 — Oil / coolant contamination** — Check fluid levels, sludge, coolant/oil mixing and visible leakage.
- **V079 — Clutch operation** — Check bite point, judder, slip and pedal effort under safe load.
- **V080 — First and second gear driving** — Test low-speed gear changes, hesitation and transmission noises.
- **V081 — Higher gears and reverse** — Check engagement, synchronisers, road speed and reverse.
- **V082 — Steering behaviour** — Check drift, vibration, tracking and return to centre.
- **V083 — Suspension on uneven road** — Listen for knocks and assess ride stability at safe speed.
- **V084 — Braking during road test** — Check stopping stability, pedal travel and unusual noises.
- **V085 — AC and ventilation** — Check AC at idle and driving speeds; blower and vents.
- **V086 — Electrical accessories** — Test power windows, locks, horn, wipers and all relevant switches.
- **V087 — OBD diagnostic scan** — Record engine/ABS/SRS fault codes; no code does not prove no past damage.
- **V088 — Complete supervised road test** — Conduct safe test with inspector where legally permitted and insured.
- **V121 — Drive belts and timing system** — Inspect applicable timing chain/belt service, accessory belts and tensioners; use OEM schedule.
- **V122 — Engine mounts and drivetrain joints** — Inspect vibrations, mount cracks, axle/CV boots, differential noises.
- **V123 — A/C coolant fan and idle loading** — Run AC at idle in traffic-like conditions; observe temperature and compressor behaviour.

## DIESEL / CNG / EV - IF APPLICABLE

- **V124 — Diesel injector, turbo and emissions components** — Check smoke, boost leaks, EGR/DPF (where fitted), injector faults and previous repairs. *(Applies: diesel)*
- **V125 — CNG approval / RTO endorsement** — Verify factory/aftermarket kit documentation and RC fuel endorsement; no unrecorded conversion. *(Applies: cng)*
- **V126 — CNG cylinder, plumbing and leaks** — Check cylinder approval/hydrotest date where required, regulator, tank mounts and gas leakage. *(Applies: cng)*
- **V127 — EV high-voltage battery state of health** — Request manufacturer diagnostic SoH and battery health history from qualified EV technician. *(Applies: ev)*
- **V128 — EV charging, BMS and cooling** — Inspect charge port, AC/DC charging, HV isolation faults and cooling loops. *(Applies: ev)*
- **V129 — Hybrid battery and regenerative braking** — Check hybrid battery diagnostics, inverter cooling and regen/physical brake function. *(Applies: hybrid)*

## INDEPENDENT INSPECTION AND TRANSACTION

- **V089 — Lift and underbody photographs** — Obtain images of mounts, crash members, corrosion and leakage.
- **V090 — Independent written inspection** — Obtain dated workshop report with engine, body and safety findings.
- **V091 — Written repair cost estimate** — Separate required mechanical/safety work from optional cosmetics.
- **V092 — Seller authority / owner identity** — Ensure seller has documents and authority for the sale.
- **V093 — Loan and legal dues** — Confirm responsibility for financier closure, taxes and pending challans.
- **V094 — Vehicle sale agreement** — Include registration, VIN, engine number, terms, date and known defects.
- **V095 — Advance/deposit conditions** — Document any advance amount and refund terms in writing.
- **V096 — Ownership transfer paperwork** — Obtain correct transfer forms and submission acknowledgement.
- **V097 — Completion of buyer name transfer** — Obtain VAHAN update/RC in buyer name, when completed.
- **V098 — Insurance transfer or new policy** — Confirm buyer insurance is effective as required.
- **V099 — Service records and keys** — Receive service bills, spare keys and supplied accessories.
- **V100 — Comparable listing evidence** — Record sources, variant, model year, mileage and asking price.
- **V130 — Inspect VIN on physical stamped metal** — Photograph physical location and compare each character to RC; do not rely only on windscreen sticker.
- **V131 — Mechanical test results and measurement evidence** — Preserve OBD codes, compression/leak down where necessary, brake tester and tyre measurements.
- **V132 — Seller declarations of modifications** — List aftermarket LPG/CNG, wheel, airbags, ECU, lights, suspension, crash repairs.
- **V133 — Receipt after transfer completion** — Capture VAHAN transfer approval and updated buyer details; an application alone is not completion.
- **V134 — Insurer policy transfer / new coverage** — Confirm effective date, coverage and named insured as applicable before road use.

## CLOSING EVIDENCE CHECKS

- **V101 — Latest approved RC expiry and status documented** — Record the result and supporting evidence before closing this item.
- **V102 — pending renewal application disposition documented** — Record the result and supporting evidence before closing this item.
- **V103 — RTO fitness inspection/test result obtained** — Record the result and supporting evidence before closing this item.
- **V104 — Any accident history indicator investigated** — Record the result and supporting evidence before closing this item.
- **V105 — Physical VIN and engine number matched to RC** — Record the result and supporting evidence before closing this item.
- **V106 — Ownership count and hypothecation verified** — Record the result and supporting evidence before closing this item.
- **V107 — Roadworthiness / safety inspection report obtained** — Record the result and supporting evidence before closing this item.
- **V108 — PUC, tax, insurance and plate status checked** — Record the result and supporting evidence before closing this item.

## Physical RTO inspection distinction

The above technical checks are a due-diligence list, not a government-certified checklist. A private car renewal (Form 25) may require an RTO vehicle inspection. Transport vehicles can require Form 38 fitness certificate. Apply category-specific rules, not all transport requirements to a private car.
