# Photo, Video, and Document Intake Protocol

## First batch — enough to start remote verification

1. Front and rear of the vehicle with readable number plates; left/right front and rear three-quarter angles; straight left/right profiles and roof.
2. Dashboard **with ignition ON but engine OFF**, then **with engine idling**; odometer and warning lamps in both states (some are normal engine-off).
3. Steering wheel hub, centre emblem, indicators/horn controls, airbags, interior seats, seat belts, door jambs, carpet, under-seat tracks, headliner and dashboard.
4. Engine compartment wide and close-up (radiator support, inner wings, strut towers, hoses/fluid caps), trunk, spare-wheel well, underbody from both ends where safe.
5. Physical VIN/chassis stamping plate and engine number stamping (ask for exact-match shot of stamping and its context; cannot infer originality from blurred photo).
6. Tyres close-ups including sidewall manufacturing date, tread and uneven wear; brake discs/drums where visible; spare, tools and jack.
7. RC front/back (owner can obscure full address/ID), insurance cover and policy dates, previous policy, PUC, servicing bills, ownership transfer forms, bank NOC/Form 35 if any, proof of last 5 years of major maintenance, RTO application receipts.
8. Original listing screenshots and seller statements on ownership, kilometers, accidents, price and reason for selling.

## Additional documents if issue appears

- Accident/insurance claim number, settlement/repair invoice, before-repair photographs, police FIR/eDAR details and body-shop job card.
- Registered owner and seller authorization (avoid posting personal IDs publicly), current RC, VIN stamped-metal photo and chassis pencil trace.
- Loan statement / lender NOC and official hypothecation termination evidence; no assumption based on 'loan cleared' statement.
- If RC renewal pending: application number, fee receipt, app status detail, inspection slot/result, approved expiry document and tax status. Do not infer completion from payment.
- For modified fuel (CNG/LPG), RTO endorsement and cylinder hydrotest/approval as required. For EV/hybrid, HV diagnostic report, charging/battery warranty and accident isolation check by qualified technician.

## Image assessment guardrails

| Visible | Acceptable report | Not acceptable inference |
|---|---|---|
| Odometer shows 60,494 | `60,494 km visible on dashboard image, capture date unknown` | `Genuine 60,494 km` |
| Toyota symbol on wheel of Maruti | `Visually appears to have another manufacturer's emblem` | `Airbag replaced because of crash` |
| Same-colour panels align | `No obvious gross misalignment in supplied angle` | `No body damage` |
| Airbag warning lamp glows with engine off | `Engine-off bulb check may be normal; take engine-idling image` | `SRS is faulty` |
| Yard appears to be scrapyard | `Vehicles/material in surrounding area; confirm seller and title` | `Vehicle salvaged` |
| Recent repaint / repair | `Mismatch in sheen/overspray/bolts; verify paint gauge/underbody` | `Major accident confirmed` |

## Safe document handling

- Keep high-resolution originals private. If sharing an assessment widely, mask owner name, address, phone, policy number and photo IDs. Retain full VIN only for authorized checks; VIN is useful but identifying.
- Do not upload private identity documents to unverified third-party history sites. Government and manufacturer portals are preferred.
- Include only needed screenshot crops in the report. When showing uncertain characters, annotate the exact uncertain span, not a guessed full value.
- For videos, note engine/cabin noise, cold vs warm start, gear engagement, warning lights and whether the recorded moment actually shows the behavior claimed.
- If a document page contains a photo or diagram, inspect the visual page, not only the extracted text.
